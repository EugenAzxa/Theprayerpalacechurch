02The threshold

Come as
you are

There is no dress code and no form to fill in at the door. Parking is free, security is on the premises, and someone is watching for you. If you are new, meet us at the Welcome Reception in the Connect Cafe straight after the Sunday service, between the north main entrance and the gymnasium.

Nursery care runs from birth to three years in Wing 2. Palace Kidz takes ages four to twelve during the 10:30 service. Youth meet Friday nights at 7:30 in the Wing 1 Chapel. Real-time translation is offered every Sunday, at no cost, in your language.

The prayer of salvation

If you prayed that, tell someone. Then take the next step: water baptism is an outward expression of an inward change, practised here by immersion. Baptism does not save a person; it is the celebration of a salvation already received.

A general in the faith

On 31 October 2024 our founder and senior pastor, Paul David Melnichuk, was promoted to glory at the age of ninety, after seventy years in the service of his Lord and Saviour. We mourn, but not as those who have no hope.

He was an unparalleled teacher, a father, and a shepherd to the people. The work of the Lord continues, as Pastor Paul would desire.

"I am the resurrection and the life. He who believes in me will live, even though he dies." John 11:25

Mission: raise the roof

Hurricane Melissa struck Jamaica on 28 October 2025 as a category five storm. In Clarendon it tore the roof clean off a local church, leaving the sanctuary open to the sky. We have committed to rebuilding it, and to reinforcing it against the next one.

08Memory and knowledge

Faith, kept
in memory

A church is not only what happens this Sunday. Sixty years on Arrow Road have left sermons, testimonies, photographs and a great deal of hard-won faith. We keep it, and we hand it on.

Sermons

Two decades of broadcasts across North America. The Word as it was preached from this platform, still there to be heard again rather than half remembered.

Testimonies

What God has done in people's lives, recorded in their own voice while they are still here to tell it.

The record of this house

Baptisms, dedications, the founding family, and the work in this city from the beginning. Sixty years of it.

The knowledge of the faith

Bible stories, memory verses and the belief statement, in a form a child and an adult can both use.

08.2Interactive learning

So the knowledge does not
stay on a shelf

Keeping a recording is not enough. What matters is that people come back to it. With Saylavy we are preparing an interactive page for this church: Bible stories, memory verses and the foundations of the faith in a living voice, with a question you can ask and an answer you can hear.

Point a phone camera at the QR code in the hall and the page opens in a second. There is nothing to install.

  • The foundations of the faith in plain words

  • A QR code in the hall, no app to install
  • In the languages this house already speaks
08.3Together with Saylavy

Memory that stays

Saylavy helps a church keep two things: the memory of its people, and the knowledge of its faith. The content is prepared and approved by the church itself, so the faith stays exactly as it is taught here.

Memory Pages

A page for each person this house remembers: a name, the years, a few lines about a life. Anyone who remembers them can add a word and pray for the family. The wall grows with the congregation.

Interactive learning

Bible stories, memory verses and the foundations of the faith in a living voice. Ask a question and hear an answer. A QR code in the hall opens it in a second.

Your own address

The church's pages live at the church's own web address, not on a shared page belonging to a service.

The pastor has the last word

Not one line appears without the church's agreement. Nothing is published here that the pastor has not approved.
